# 👗 Lucy — Your Personal Stylist

> **Shopping should feel personal again.**

Lucy is an AI-powered personal stylist that understands your unique fashion preferences, curates complete outfits, helps you visualize them before purchasing, and manages your shopping journey from discovery to delivery.

Unlike traditional shopping assistants that stop after recommending products, Lucy is designed as an **Agentic Commerce Assistant**—helping users make confident purchasing decisions while remaining involved throughout the entire shopping lifecycle.

---

## ✨ Vision

Finding the perfect outfit shouldn't require hours of scrolling through thousands of products.

Lucy learns your personal style, understands your budget and occasion, recommends complete looks instead of individual products, explains every recommendation, and stays with you until your order arrives.

Our goal is simple:

> **Make online shopping feel like having a personal stylist available 24/7.**

---

# 🚀 Features

### 🎯 Personalized Style Profile

Lucy learns your:

* Favorite colors
* Fashion styles
* Budget
* Preferred brands
* Shopping habits
* Occasions
* Style preferences

Every recommendation becomes more personalized over time.

---

### 👗 AI Outfit Curation

Instead of recommending a single product, Lucy creates complete looks.

Example:

* Dress
* Shoes
* Bag
* Accessories
* Jewelry

Everything is selected to work together.

---

### 🔍 Intelligent Product Discovery

Lucy searches across supported merchants to find products that best match your preferences.

Recommendations are based on:

* Personal style
* Budget
* Occasion
* Product quality
* Ratings
* Availability

—not just popularity.

---

### 💡 Explainable Recommendations

Every recommendation includes clear reasoning.

Example:

* ✅ Matches your minimalist style
* ✅ Suitable for warm weather
* ✅ Fits your budget
* ✅ Highly rated
* ✅ Fast delivery

Lucy explains *why* each product was selected.

---

### 📸 Virtual Try-On

Preview how selected outfits look before purchasing.

Reduce uncertainty and shop with confidence.

---

### 💳 Agentic Commerce

Lucy doesn't simply recommend products.

She helps complete the shopping experience.

Workflow:

```
Understand User
        ↓
Search Products
        ↓
Compare Options
        ↓
Build Complete Outfit
        ↓
Virtual Try-On
        ↓
Purchase Approval
        ↓
Payment
        ↓
Order Confirmation
        ↓
Delivery Tracking
```

---

### 📦 Order Tracking

Lucy remains available after checkout.

Track:

* Order confirmation
* Shipment updates
* Delivery status
* Estimated arrival

Shopping doesn't end at payment.

---

### 🔒 Privacy & Trust

Lucy is built around transparency.

* Purchases always require user approval.
* Personal preferences remain private.
* Every recommendation is explainable.
* Users stay in control throughout the shopping process.
